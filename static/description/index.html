Polish VAT White List

Check a vendor's bank account against the Ministry of Finance White List (Biała Lista) - in one click, right from the bill.

The problem

In Poland, paying a vendor into a bank account that is not on the Ministry of Finance White List can cost you the tax deduction and make you jointly liable for the vendor's VAT. Checking means copying the NIP and account number into the government website for every bill - and keeping proof that you did it.

How it works

1

Open a vendor bill

With the vendor's NIP and Recipient Bank account filled in.

2

Click "Verify on White List"

Odoo asks the official MF API for the bill date.

3

See the answer, keep the proof

A status badge next to the account, and the MF request ID saved on the bill.

What you get

Proof of verification

Every check is logged on the bill with the MF request ID, the date checked, who checked it and the full API response.

Status at a glance

A green, red or orange badge next to Recipient Bank. Change the account and the badge clears until the new one is checked.

Never in your way

A negative or failed check doesn't block the bill - you get a clear warning and decide what to do.

Audit-safe log

Accountants can run checks but not edit or delete them - only accounting managers can.

Where it applies

Vendor bills and vendor refunds with a Polish NIP and a Polish (26-digit) bank account. Requires the Invoicing / Accounting app and internet access to the public MF API - no API key, no configuration.
